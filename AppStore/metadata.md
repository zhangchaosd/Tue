# Tue 1.3 App Store Metadata

## Promotional Text

**English (U.S.)**

Tue now speaks your language. A refreshed native design, a new Liquid Glass icon, one-tap SSH commands and hidden-by-default passwords keep your hosts close and safe.

**简体中文**

Tue 现已支持简体中文！全新原生设计与 Liquid Glass 图标，一键拷贝 SSH 命令，密码默认隐藏，查找和使用服务器信息更快也更安心。

## What's New in 1.3

**English (U.S.)**

- Simplified Chinese support. Tue follows your system language automatically.
- A brand-new app icon designed for Liquid Glass.
- Every account now has a ready-to-paste SSH command.
- Passwords stay hidden until you tap the eye button.
- Long-press a host to copy its IP, domain, username or SSH command.
- Choose a color and an icon for each label.
- Polished layouts, clearer empty states, inline validation for required fields, and haptic feedback when copying.

**简体中文**

- 新增简体中文，跟随系统语言自动切换。
- 全新设计的 Liquid Glass 风格图标。
- 每个账户都可以直接拷贝拼好的 SSH 命令。
- 密码默认隐藏，轻点眼睛按钮即可显示。
- 长按主机即可拷贝 IP、域名、用户名或 SSH 命令。
- 可以为每个标签选择颜色和图标。
- 界面细节全面打磨：更清晰的空状态、必填项即时提示，拷贝时带触感反馈。
